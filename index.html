Welcome to FSKTM IT Club

The FSKTM IT Club brings students together to explore technology, develop computing skills and share ideas. We encourage teamwork, creativity and continuous learning in information technology.

Events and Activities

Our activities include coding workshops, cybersecurity awareness sessions, technology talks and competitions. These activities help students improve their technical skills and prepare for future careers in the IT industry.
